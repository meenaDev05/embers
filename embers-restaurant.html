Our story

Built around one fire, not a hundred appliances.

Embers started with a single question: what happens if a kitchen has no fryers, no microwaves, no shortcuts — just wood, coal, and people who know how to read a flame.

Every dish that leaves the kitchen has touched the fire in some way, whether it's the char on a flatbread or the smoke folded into a dessert.
